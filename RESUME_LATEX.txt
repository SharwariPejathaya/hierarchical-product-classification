\resumeItemListStart
\resumeItem{Engineered multi-task learning architecture using DistilBERT embeddings with custom classification heads and GECE (Global-local hierarchically Enhanced Classification with taxonomy Enforcement) hybrid classifier combining global cross-entropy supervision with local hierarchical constraints for taxonomy-aware e-commerce product categorization across 5 category levels (L0-L4) and 4,656 brands.}
\resumeItem{Achieved 45$\times$ inference speedup through strategic embedding layer freezing optimization while maintaining 98\% brand accuracy and 88-91\% hierarchical accuracy across multiple classification levels in production deployment using precomputed 768-dimensional DistilBERT embeddings.}
\resumeItem{Implemented masked inference mechanism with parent-child taxonomy enforcement preventing logically impossible classification paths through multi-head classification architecture enabling simultaneous predictions across product hierarchy levels (L0→L1→L2→L3→L4→Brand) using constraint dictionaries for valid class indices.}
\resumeItem{Developed BERT-tiny hierarchical classifier with constraint masking achieving 97.16\% L0 accuracy and 98.62\% brand accuracy through end-to-end transformer fine-tuning with DataParallel training on dual T4 GPUs, multi-task loss optimization ($L = \sum_{i=0}^{4} L_{Li} + L_{brand}$), and hierarchical relationship enforcement.}
\resumeItem{Conducted ablation studies on training time vs. accuracy trade-offs comparing end-to-end BERT fine-tuning (15 hours, 88-98\% accuracy) against frozen embedding approaches (20 minutes, 80-95\% accuracy), developed evaluation methodology for structured prediction problems with 10 L0 categories, 67 L1, 292 L2, 944 L3, and 2173 L4 categories.}
